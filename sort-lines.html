Put a jumbled list in order. Numbers inside words are compared as numbers, so “item2” comes before “item10”.

Your text stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored by this site.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste one item per line.
  2. Pick A to Z or Z to A and your options.
  3. Copy or download the sorted list.

Example

Input banana, Apple, cherry sorts to Apple, banana, cherry with case-insensitive sorting. With number-aware sorting, item2 comes before item10.

Frequently asked questions

What is number-aware sorting?

Digit runs are compared as whole numbers, so version and file names sort in the order a person expects.

What does case sensitive do?

When ticked, uppercase letters sort separately from lowercase. When unticked, “Apple” and “apple” sort together.

Can I sort and remove duplicates at once?

Yes. Tick Remove duplicate lines before sorting; the first copy of each line is kept.
