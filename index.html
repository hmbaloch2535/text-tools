Free Text Tools That Work in Your Browser

TextKit is a small collection of free, fast text tools. Paste your text, get an instant result, and copy it back out. No sign-up, no installation, and your text is processed on your own device - it is not uploaded or stored by this site.

Why use TextKit?

  • Instant: results appear as you type or with one click.
  • Private by design: all processing happens in your browser with plain JavaScript.
  • Free: no account, no payment, no download.
  • Mobile friendly: every tool works on a phone, tablet or desktop.

Who are these tools for?

Students checking an essay word limit, writers tidying a draft, developers cleaning a list, marketers preparing keywords, and anyone who needs a quick, reliable text fix. Each tool page explains what it does, shows an example, and answers common questions.

Frequently asked questions

Is TextKit really free?

Yes. The tools on this site are free to use, with no account required.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The tools run in your browser using JavaScript. Closing the page ends the session; this site does not store the text you paste.

Do the tools work on a phone?

Yes. The layout is responsive, so text boxes and buttons adapt to smaller screens.
