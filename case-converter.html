Frequently asked questions

What does Title Case do?

It lowercases the text, then capitalises the first letter of each word. It does not know style-guide exceptions for small words such as “and” or “of”.

What does Sentence Case do?

It lowercases the text, then capitalises the first letter at the start and after sentence-ending punctuation.

Will it change names or acronyms?

Yes, if you convert to lowercase or sentence case. Check proper names and acronyms after converting.
