Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Text is trimmed, then split on whitespace. A word is a run of non-whitespace characters, so punctuation attached to a word stays part of that word.

What is the difference between characters with and without spaces?

With spaces counts every character including spaces and line breaks. Without spaces removes all whitespace first, which is the count many editors and platforms ask for.

Does the counter handle other languages?

Yes. It splits on whitespace and counts Unicode code points, so accented letters and emoji are handled as single characters in the character count.
