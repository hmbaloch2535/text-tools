Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. This policy describes what this site does and does not collect.

Text you paste into tools

Tools on this site run in your browser using JavaScript. Your pasted text is not uploaded to, stored on, or sold by this site. Closing the page ends your session.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics tool and no advertising code is included in this build yet. If analytics or advertising is added later, this policy must be updated before that version is published, stating what each service collects.

Cookies

This static build does not set its own cookies. A hosting provider or an advertising network added later may set its own cookies; that must be disclosed here before publication.

Contact

See the Contact page. No contact email is published until the site owner adds a real one at deployment.
