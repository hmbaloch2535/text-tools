How to use this tool

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Read the with-spaces and without-spaces counts.
  3. Use letters, digits and line counts to check form limits.

Example

Input: “Hi there!” gives 9 characters with spaces and 8 without spaces.

Frequently asked questions

Why do with-spaces and without-spaces counts differ?

Spaces, tabs and line breaks are characters too. Removing them gives a shorter count, which some limits use.

How are emoji counted?

As one character each, because the count uses Unicode code points rather than raw UTF-16 units.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser.
