How to use this tool

  1. Type or paste a title.
  2. Press Generate slug.
  3. Copy the slug and use it in your URL.

Example

Input “My First Blog Post!” becomes “my-first-blog-post”. Accented letters are converted to their plain equivalents where possible.

Frequently asked questions

What characters are allowed in a slug?

Lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. Spaces and punctuation become hyphens, and repeated hyphens are collapsed.

What happens to accents?

Letters are normalised and accent marks are removed, so “café” becomes “cafe”.

Can a slug be empty?

If the input has no letters or digits, the slug is empty. Add a word or number and try again.
