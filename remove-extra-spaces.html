Frequently asked questions

Does it remove single spaces between words?

No. Only repeated spaces and tabs are collapsed to one space, and each line is trimmed.

Does it remove line breaks?

No. Line breaks are kept, so paragraphs and lists keep their structure.

What about blank lines?

With the option ticked, three or more blank lines in a row become one blank line.
