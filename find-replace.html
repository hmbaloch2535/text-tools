Find and Replace Text Online - Free Replace Tool | TextKit

Replace every occurrence of a word or phrase at once, instead of editing each one by hand.

Your text stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored by this site.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Type what to find and what to replace it with.
  3. Press Replace all and check the replacement count.

Example

Finding “colour” and replacing it with “color” changes every “colour” in the text. With Case sensitive unticked, “Colour” is also replaced.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if Find is empty?

Nothing is replaced. The tool asks you to enter text to find first.

What is the pattern option?

It treats Find as a regular expression, for advanced users. An invalid pattern shows an error instead of changing your text.

Does it replace only the first match?

No. Replace all replaces every match in the text.
