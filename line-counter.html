Count lines in a list, a document or pasted data, and see the longest and average line length.

Your text stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored by this site.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste text with one item per line.
  2. Read total, non-empty and empty line counts.
  3. Use the longest-line note to spot unusually long entries.

Example

Input with three lines, one of them blank, gives 3 total lines, 2 non-empty lines and 1 empty line.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a line?

Text separated by a line break. A trailing line break does not create an extra counted line in the way this tool splits text.

What is an empty line?

A line with no characters, or only whitespace, once trimmed.

Does it count words too?

No. Use the Word Counter page for word and character counts.
